# LOVE TO RUIN — The Story

The whole story, start to finish: what's already in the game, and everything still to come, on all three paths.
`DESIGN.md` covers how the game works; this file covers what happens and why.

**Spoilers for everything.** Things marked **(built)** are already in the game.

---

## The story in one breath

A wanderer with purple skin and no past walks into San Diego, picks up a warm red shard that hums like it's breathing, and makes a friend named Hop.
The shards are pieces of **Hopkuna**, a monster who lives inside Hop.
Five years ago, Hop's best friend **Relic** tore Hopkuna's power out of him and died doing it.
Every shard is Hopkuna's power wrapped around a piece of Relic.
Elric gathers them, and three things want them: the **REVOLUTION Corps** (to destroy them), **Hopkuna** (to be whole), and **Relic** (who wakes up inside Elric and wants something different on every path).

The secret at the center of the story: **Elric is Relic's last keepsake.** In the moment Relic broke apart, they took the one thing they'd never let anyone carry for them, their wish for a home, and kept it safe in the only shape big enough to hold it: a person. Elric has been walking toward that home for five years without knowing it.

## 1. What the story is about

| Thread | What it means | Where it pays off |
|---|---|---|
| **Carrying it alone** | Relic carried everyone's pain by themself, and it killed them. Nassan: *"Don't carry it alone."* | Pacifist: thirteen friends share the weight. Genocide: Relic stops carrying and starts taking. |
| **Letting go** | KEEPSAKE only works on what someone truly wants gone: *"You can't pull a splinter out of a fist that won't open."* | Every ending turns on who lets go of what. Hop had to let go of Hopkuna once; now he has to let go of Relic. |
| **Home** | Elric never stays anywhere, and doesn't know why. | Pacifist: the Corps becomes home. Neutral: the road. Genocide: Elric destroys any chance of one. |
| **Three of us** | The tent: *"We were here, too. Three of us."* Green, red and purple eyes: Relic, Hopkuna, Elric. | The last reveal: all three were on that field that night. |
| **LOVE vs. BOND** | LOVE: Level Of ViolencE. BOND: Bound by Our New Determination (kept secret until the Pacifist finale). | The title: **LOVE** to ruin, **BOND** to save. |
| **DETERMINATION** | Hopkuna remembers your RESETs. So, eventually, does Relic. | The fourth wall cracks a little more each time you reset. |

## 3. Part One: the opening (built)

Everything here is in the game. It's here so the rest connects.

**The voice.**
Before anything, a voice with no face tells Elric the goal (find the twelve fragments) and the two ways to grow (TALK and SPARE for BOND, FIGHT for LOVE), then asks: *"Here is your objective. How will you do it?"*
It's Hopkuna, though nobody knows that yet. If you reset, he remembers.

**Mt. Carmel High.**
Elric meets Hop: *"Elric. ...Huh. Sorry. You just remind me of someone."*
They find the first fragment by the bleachers. Eggo and Big Joe catch Elric holding it, take them for Hopkuna's lackey, and attack (the tutorial fight).

**The PQ Mall.**
The cast hangs out between adventures.
- Nat tells the old story of twelve pieces and *"somebody who gave everything to break it apart."* The last page of his book is torn out. Hop goes still.
- Nassan sends them to Westview after dark, and starts his new job at Vons.
- Rock Paper Scissors with N.C. Wethan, Ronin and Agent, and the day turns to night.

**Westview High.**
- The endless hallway, the bell puzzle, and Wally Wolverine.
- Fragment 2 floats out of the empty costume. Hop's shadow looks wrong when he reaches for it.
- Arriving, Hop slips: *"Rel-- Elric. Stay close."*
- If you're unlucky, you meet the tent: *"DID YOU THINK WE WOULD FORGET?"* Then two green eyes, and the wind.

**Westview Field.**
- Fragment 3 erupts at Elric, and Hop takes the blast.
- Hopkuna wakes: *"...Finally."* You can't win; you can only survive.
- The REVOLUTION Corps surrounds him: *"Thirteen of us. One of you. Do the math."*
- Elric says *"Come back,"* and Hop does.
- Hopkuna leaves a promise: *"Three fragments, little wanderer. Nine to go. I can wait."*

**The choice:** Join the REVOLUTION Corps. Go my own way. Go with Hop.

**Right after it (built):**
- **Corps:** down the hatch into the bunker. Elric gets a bunk with a sign on it ("ELRIC", the E backwards).
- **Own way:** Elric wanders all night, and their feet bring them back to the field by morning.
- **With Hop:** the glowbug (the first death), Hop's house, the photo with one face rubbed away, and the mirror: *"It's me, RELIC."*

## 4. The rest of the map: fragments 4 to 12

Every fragment is a new place in San Diego with:
- a **guardian** (something ordinary brought to life by the fragment, like Wally)
- a **Corps member's story**
- a **keepsake memory**

**Keepsake memories** are the spine of the story. Touching a fragment plays a short scene from Relic's life, tinted green, where you play as Relic. Played in order, they tell the summer of Relic and Hop, up to the fire, and then the one thing nobody knows.
On every path, the fragments play their memories in the same order (by how many you've gathered, not where you found them), so the story always unfolds right.

### The guardians, briefly
Each is a miniboss with its own song, a gimmick, and a way to spare it, the way Wally was.

- **The Dipper:** the fight happens on the moving coaster. The battle box tilts on the drops and attacks come with the track's rhythm (clack-clack-clack, DROP). Spare it by riding it properly: ACT "Hands Up" on every drop.
- **The Empty Knight:** a duel. Big Joe insists on fighting it himself, by the rules of a fair joust. Spare it by refusing to hit it while it's down. It's an empty suit of armor that has only ever wanted a worthy opponent.
- **The Hostess:** she won't fight until you sit down to dinner. Her attacks are courses: soup waves, a carving-knife rain, dessert. Spare it by complimenting each course. She's been setting the table for guests who never came, for 150 years.
- **The Big Screen:** shows replays of your own last turn and fires them back at you. Supreme reads its patterns out loud. Spare it by doing the wave with the crowd that isn't there.
- **Flight Deck:** a two-part fight. Agent calls the dodges, which come with a beat of warning. Spare it by guiding it in to land, a timing ACT.
- **The Glider:** in the open sky, the box drifts on the wind. Spare it by convincing it that coming down isn't the same as giving up. Rooster has to admit he's scared of heights to help.
- **Ember:** the hardest guardian. Pure fire, the same fire. Hop can't be on the team for this one. Ronin learns to hold his flame still instead of throwing it. Spare it by letting it burn out: survive, don't feed it.
- **Scrap Heap:** it flings old cars. One of them is a van that's been here for years, and one of Sansworth's 31 keys starts it. (He has been looking for "his car" the whole game. It was always this one, abandoned by an uncle he forgot he had.)

## 5. Pacifist: Join the REVOLUTION Corps

**Elric finds a home.** The Corps stops being a team and becomes a family, and BOND, not LOVE, makes Elric strong.

### Act 1: The bunker (after the choice)
- **Life in the base (built):** rooms, conversations, Gerald the training dummy, the bunk dream. In the dream, the voice speaks for the first time: *"The pieces. I can feel every one of them."*
- **The first keepsake memories.** The three fragments Elric already has play their memories together, the first night in the bunk: a road at dawn, a gym at night, curly fries. Elric wakes up holding nothing, very tight.
- **Hop is on probation.** Big Joe wants him watched. Agent wants him locked up: *"The host is the risk. Remove the risk."* Eggo shrugs: *"...you could also just talk to him."* (A callback to the tutorial.) Hop sleeps on the couch in the main hall and pretends he doesn't hear them.
- **The plan.** Nassan's wall: twelve circles, nine left. How to destroy a fragment: N.C. Wethan's lightning and Ronin's fire together can crack one, because it took a fire to make them. Each fragment goes back to the crater on Westview Field to be broken. Nassan: *"Step one: find them. Step two: we'll figure out step two."*

### Act 2: The summer (fragments 4 to 7)
A free-roaming stretch. Each fragment is a day trip with a different pair of Corps members, so the player gets to know everyone.
- **Mission Beach:** Crayola opens up for the first time. N.C. Wethan can't swim well with lightning in him: *"I CONDUCT. IT'S A WHOLE THING."* After the Dipper, Crayola gives Elric his card: the **Seven of Hearts**, from the card trick at the mall. It's a BOND item: "Someone believes in you."
- **Balboa Park:** Big Joe vs. the Empty Knight. In the museum archives, Nat finds a second copy of his book. Its last page is there, and it says: **"...gave everything to break it apart. Their name was Relic."** Nat realizes who tore out his copy's page. He tells only Elric, and asks them to let Hop say it himself.
- **Old Town:** the Hostess, and Nat's quiet arc: he knows too much history to get attached to anything, and he gets attached to the Corps anyway. The keepsake memory reveals that **Relic's collection was the townsfolk's pain**. A side quest opens: **Relic's lost collection**, the bottle cap, the cracked compass, the chess piece and the rest, scattered around town. Each one belongs to a townsperson, and returning it gives them back something they lost. The Pacifist path asks a harder question about these: see Act 4.
- **Downtown:** Supreme runs the numbers on beating Hopkuna: **0.4%**. He stays anyway. *"Statistically, I should leave. Emotionally, I'm staying. I didn't know I had that column."*

**Breaking a fragment.** Each one goes back to the crater, where lightning and fire crack it. The red burns away, and for a moment there's a green light inside, like a person curled up asleep. Then it's gone, and the wind picks up across the field.

### Act 3: The Harbor, and the split (fragment 8)
- On the old aircraft carrier, the keepsake memory is *"If you ever want him gone, I could try."* Hop says no. Elric watches Hop's past self say it.
- **Hop tells Elric everything.** On the flight deck at night: Relic, the summer, the tent, the fire, what he did, and what it cost. *"You look like them. You walk like them. You stand on the edge of things like them. I couldn't tell you. I didn't want you to be a replacement. I wanted you to be you."*
- **The vote.** Agent calls a meeting: the risk is mathematically clear, so seal Hop away. Big Joe is torn between justice and his friend. The Corps splits. **Elric's choices here matter:** they can speak for Hop at the vote, and what they say (and how much BOND they have with each member) decides how the vote falls.
- **However it falls, Hop leaves that night.** He takes nothing and leaves a note on the couch: *"Don't carry me. You've got enough."*

### Act 4: Torrey Pines, and the turn (fragment 9)
- Elric finds Hop at the cliffs, where the Glider won't come down. Rooster has to fly. Rooster is terrified, and the bravest he's ever been.
- **Hopkuna strikes.** Hop is exhausted and alone. Hopkuna takes him over, beats the whole team on the cliffs (a fight you lose, like the field), and steals the fragments the Corps hasn't broken yet. *"You've been breaking my things. Rude."*
- Hop is gone, and so is Hopkuna. The base is quiet.
- **The keepsakes come home.** With most of Relic's collection returned, the townsfolk start to change: Pigeon Man cries at the pigeons, the Waiting Ghost finally goes home, Coach Ramirez remembers why he lost his keys in the first place. **Their pain is coming back to them.** Relic was carrying it, and breaking the fragments and returning the keepsakes is giving it back. This is the Pacifist path's real question: freeing Relic means the city has to carry its own grief again.
- **The answer is BOND.** One by one, Corps members go out and sit with the townsfolk who got their pain back. Nobody carries it alone. Elric walks the town, and every person they've spared and helped has someone next to them. (This is where all the "Talk" and "Spare" choices across the game come back.)

### Act 5: The fire (fragments 10 and 11)
- **The burned hills:** Ember, the fire's leftover heart. Ronin learns to hold his fire still. The keepsake memory is the tent: *"Room for three."*
- **The junkyard:** Sansworth finds his van. It actually runs: *"I TOLD you I had a car."* (He didn't know he had a car.) The memory is the fire itself, and **"Hop. Let go."**
- Elric now knows almost everything. Except why the twelfth fragment was never on any map.

### Act 6: Fragment 12, and the last keepsake
- Nassan finally says what he's known since the beginning: one of the twelve circles on his map was always on **Hop's house.** He never said so, because he couldn't plan around his friend lying to him.
- At Hop's empty house, in the jar of bottle caps labeled KEEP (in Relic's handwriting), is **fragment 12**. Hop found it in the burned field at dawn, five years ago. He never broke it and never told anyone. It's the biggest piece of Relic, and it's warm. He couldn't let go.
- Elric picks it up, and the last keepsake plays:

#### The last keepsake
> The fire. Relic, breaking apart with all of Hopkuna's power in their hands.
> Twelve pieces, scattered on the wind.
> And one more thing, that Relic never put in any object, because they never let anyone carry it for them: **their wish for a home.**
> Relic keeps it the only way they know how. They put it into a keepsake.
> But a wish that big doesn't fit in a bottle cap or a token.
> It needs a shape that can walk.
>
> Somewhere outside San Diego, at dawn, on an empty road, someone with purple skin opens their eyes for the first time.
> They don't remember anything. They just know they have to keep walking.
> *(It's Elric.)*

- **Elric is not Relic.** Elric is what Relic wanted most, and couldn't keep: a home. That's why Elric has no past, why they wander, why the fragments called them, and why Hop saw his friend in them.
- The voice in the dreams finally says it: *"It's me. Relic. I made you. I'm sorry I made you lonely. I didn't know how to make anything else."*

### Act 7: Westview Field, the final fight
- Hopkuna has the fragments he stole on the cliffs, burning inside Hop's body. It isn't enough to make him whole, but it's enough to make him terrifying. He needs the twelfth. He goes to the crater, where it all started, and calls Elric out.
- **The whole Corps comes.** In Sansworth's van, all twelve of them, the way they formed the circle before. Agent: *"Thirteen of us. One of you. I already did the math."*
- **The final battle: Hopkuna, Unbound.** Unlike the field, this one you can win, but not by fighting. Every Corps member can be called, every one with their move. Hopkuna's attacks are everything from the game: Cleave, the Closing Ring, the fire. The battle turns on **ACT: Talk to Hop**, which finally works, a little more each time, as every friend adds their voice.
- **The reveal of BOND.** At the lowest point, Elric's HP at 1, the screen shows the stat: **BOND**, and then what it means: **Bound by Our New Determination.** Every Corps member puts a hand on Elric's shoulder. The SOUL turns from red to something brighter.
- **"Hop. Let go."** Elric says it, in Relic's words, with Relic's voice underneath. This time Hop isn't alone when he does. He lets go of Hopkuna, and of Relic. The twelve friends hold Elric up while Elric does what Relic did, but doesn't break: they keep Hopkuna in the **curly-fry token**, the last thing in Relic's collection, now holding the thing Relic died trying to hold. It doesn't break, because this time thirteen people are carrying it.
- **Hopkuna's last words** (from inside the token, quiet for the first time): *"...It's dark in here." "Yeah," says Hop. "I know. I'll talk to you sometimes."*

### The ending
- The last fragment breaks in the crater. The green light inside unfolds into the shape of a kid in a green hoodie, standing next to Elric.
- **Relic says goodbye to Hop**, through Elric: *"It wasn't your fault. I told you to let go. You did. That's the bravest thing I ever saw."*
- **To Elric:** *"You found it. Didn't you? ...Keep it."* Then the wind, the same wind, warm this time, and they're gone.
- **Epilogue:** a month later. The field is green again. A new three-person tent, and the whole Corps crammed around it, way more than three. Hop wears the token on a cord around his neck. The bunk sign has been fixed: someone turned the E the right way round.
- **Last line**, Elric's, the most they've ever said at once: *"...I think I'll stay."*
- The ring of twelve pieces from the opening, every one of them gone, and in the middle, a small purple light that doesn't move. **HOME.**

## 6. Neutral: Go my own way

**Elric keeps walking.** The fragments pull, and Relic's voice says follow. The Corps hunts Elric for the fragments. So does Hopkuna. Elric belongs to neither.

### How it differs
- **Elric fights alone**, at least at first. No partner, no calls. It's harder, and lonelier, on purpose. (Wild encounters and challenge fights hit harder without a friend.)
- **The Corps' offer stays open until the Harbor (fragment 8).** At any point before then, Elric can go down the hatch and join. The path becomes Pacifist from there (built: the guest arrival, *"Fine. But you're on dish duty."*). After the Harbor, the hatch is locked: *"The offer stood. It doesn't anymore."* (Nassan's note, taped to it.)
- **The keepsake memories play, but Elric watches them alone.** Nobody tells Elric who Relic is. They piece it together from the memories, and from Hop, who keeps showing up.
- **Hop drifts between.** He stays with the Corps, but he keeps finding Elric: sitting on a bench at the boardwalk, at the edge of the cliffs. He's the one person who talks to Elric on this path, and he tells them the truth about Relic at Torrey Pines, because they're alone and he can't not.

### The story
- **Fragments 4 to 9:** Elric gets to each one before the Corps, or races them for it. Some guardians the Corps already fought, and Elric arrives to find them spared (or worse). Elric meets each Corps member as a rival rather than a friend: Big Joe challenges Elric to a duel for a fragment (spare-able), Agent tries to out-think them, Supreme calculates their odds, out loud, unhelpfully.
- **Relic's lost collection** is the heart of this path. Elric finds the keepsakes alone and decides what to do with each: **give it back** (the townsperson's pain comes back, and Elric stays and sits with them, a small BOND), or **keep it** (Elric carries it, like Relic did, and the backpack gets a little heavier: a tiny permanent stat cost). It's a choice about whether Elric becomes Relic.
- **Torrey Pines:** the voice of Relic, gentle but tired: *"You keep walking away from people. I did that too."* Hop tells Elric everything, on the cliff, and says he wishes Elric had come with them.
- **Fragments 10 to 12:** the burned hills, the junkyard and Hop's jar. Elric gets there on their own or after the Corps, depending on how they've played. The last keepsake plays: **Elric learns what they are, alone.**

### The final choice: Westview Field
Hopkuna and the Corps both come to the field for Elric's fragments. Elric stands in the crater. Whoever has the most of the twelve decides; if Elric has any, they choose:

| Ending | What Elric does | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **The Road** | Keeps the fragments and walks away. | The most Neutral ending. Elric leaves San Diego with Relic's pieces in the backpack, and the pull never stops. Credits over the endless road from the opening. Relic, quietly: *"Keep walking."* Hopkuna is still in Hop, waiting. Nothing is solved; everyone is still out there. |
| **The Gift** | Gives the fragments to the Corps, and leaves. | The Corps breaks them. Relic is freed, and says goodbye to Hop, but Elric isn't there to hear it. Hop looks for Elric on the road for a long time. Bittersweet. |
| **The Bargain** | Gives the fragments to Hopkuna. | Hopkuna wins. The city burns. Elric walks away and doesn't look back. Hopkuna's last line, to the player: *"You didn't even pick a side. You just didn't care. ...That's worse."* |

**Epilogue, like Undertale's phone call:** for each Corps member and townsperson, a line about how they're doing, decided by what Elric did to them: spared, helped, killed, ignored, kept their keepsake or gave it back. If Elric gave back every keepsake, the last line is from Pigeon Man: *"Some kid gave me back the worst year of my life. Funny thing. I'm glad they did."*

## 7. Genocide: Go with Hop

**Relic stops carrying and starts taking.** It begins with a glowbug that couldn't fight back, and ends with the thing Relic was always afraid they'd become.

### What's different about this path
- From the glowbug on, the narrator drops the parentheses and says **"we"** (built).
- Elric's attacks are Relic's: green, doubled, 1851293 on the first (built).
- Dread grows with every kill, and Elric's body turns into Relic's (built): green eyes, then the smile, then Relic in the flesh. At 75 kills Elric *is* Relic, to anyone who looks.
- People run, hide and peek (built). The city empties as you kill.
- **Hopkuna becomes the only one telling the truth.** The villain sees exactly what's happening, and it scares him.

### Act 1: Morning (fragments 4 to 6)
- Pancakes at Hop's (built). Hop gives Elric his only plate. *"Your eyes are... green? Were they always...? Ha. Weird lighting."*
- Out the door, into the city, with Hop. Hopkuna wants the fragments, and Hop, wanting to protect Elric, goes along. Relic wants them more.
- **The guardians aren't spared on this path.** They can only be killed. The Dipper derails; the Empty Knight falls apart, empty; the Hostess's table is overturned.
- The keepsake memories still play, but **Relic narrates them now**, bitter: *"See how happy we were? Then they let us burn."*
- **The townsfolk vanish.** Each kill is permanent. The mall's shops close one by one (built: the notes; on this path they appear as each shopkeeper flees, not all at once). Nassan's note is the last to go up.

### Act 2: The Corps fights back (fragments 7 to 9)
- On this path, **the Corps members can be fought, and they come for you.** Each is a real boss, the way Undertale's Genocide route has its guardians:
  - **Supreme** (downtown): a bullet-hell made of his statistics, every attack labeled with its odds of hitting you. He knows the odds of winning are zero. He shows up anyway.
  - **Agent** (the Harbor): the hardest pure-skill fight in the game. He predicts your movement, like the Rock Paper Scissors round, and plays the counter. *"Three moves ahead. You're only one."*
  - **Rooster** (Torrey Pines): he roasts you through the whole fight, and the jokes fall apart as he gets scared. His last line isn't a joke.
  - **Big Joe and Eggo, together:** the tutorial fight again, but real. Eggo's last pun lands perfectly, and nobody laughs.
  - Every Corps member has a fight, or a moment where they stand in the way. Some can be avoided by moving fast; none can be spared.
- **Nassan's last stand is at Vons.** He's working the register. He doesn't fight; he hands you the note, five pages, and asks you to read it in front of him. Then he steps aside. *"I told you not to carry it alone."* Elric can still kill him. (Nassan is the one fight the game lets you walk away from, and the dialogue changes forever if you do.)

### Act 3: "Relic? You came back?"
- At **75 kills**, Elric is Relic in the flesh. Wherever that happens, Hop sees.
- **Hop is happy.** Five years of grief, and his best friend is standing in front of him, green hoodie, curly-fry token on its cord. *"...Relic? You came back? You came BACK."* He'll follow Relic anywhere. He's not a prisoner. He walks in willingly.
- **Hopkuna, inside, is the only one who sees it:** *"That isn't them. Relic would never smile like that."* Hop doesn't listen. For the first time in his life, Hopkuna is the one begging Hop to let him out, to fight, to run. Hop says no.
- (If you never reach 75 kills on this path, Hop never mistakes Elric for Relic. He just gets quieter and quieter, and the endings change: see below.)

### Act 4: The last fragments (10 to 12)
- **The burned hills:** Ember isn't a guardian on this path. It's Relic's fire, recognizing them. It doesn't fight. It lets them through.
- **The junkyard:** Sansworth is waiting at the van. He finally found his car, and he was going to drive everyone to safety. He's the last of the Corps. He's an idiot to the very end and doesn't run. (The van never starts.)
- **Hop gives them the jar** himself. *"I kept it for you. I always kept it for you."* The last keepsake plays, but Relic has already read it. *"A home. Ha. We don't need a home. We have everything."*

### The end: Westview Field
- Twelve fragments. Hopkuna, at last: *"Give them to me. That was the deal."* He has waited five years for this.
- **Relic turns on Hopkuna.** They do what they did the night of the fire: pull all of Hopkuna's power out of Hop. This time they don't break. They keep it, because they've stopped caring what it costs.
- **The final battle is Hopkuna fighting for his life.** He's the underdog now: desperate, furious, scared. His attacks are everything Relic ever had to dodge. He loses. *"You were supposed to be MINE. You were MY little wanderer."* Relic: *"We were never anyone's."*
- **Hop, alone in the crater with his friend:** *"Relic. Please. Let go."*
- Relic: *"I let go of everything, my whole life. Everyone else's pain. Now it's my turn to keep something."*
- **Relic keeps Hop.** KEEPSAKE, one last time: Hop, all of him, into the curly-fry token. The token goes dark. Relic puts it on its cord around their neck.
- **The Santa Ana wind** comes in hot off the hills. The city goes orange. Credits don't roll. A green-eyed figure stands alone in the burned field. **Three of us** appears in the dark, letter by letter: one pair of green eyes, and nothing else.

### Variations
- **Under 75 kills:** Relic never fully takes Elric's body. At the end, when Relic turns on Hopkuna, Elric is still in there, and gets one choice: **let go.** If Elric lets go, Relic fades, and Elric collapses on the field next to Hop, who stays with them. A harsh, quiet ending: nothing fixed, but not lost either. Hopkuna is gone too, broken apart in the struggle. (This is the "you didn't go all the way" ending, the closest Genocide gets to mercy.)
- **Nassan spared at Vons:** in the final scene, a second set of footsteps. Nassan, at the edge of the field, says one thing before the screen goes dark: *"...I'm still here."* It changes nothing. It's still there.

## 8. Resetting, DETERMINATION, and the second playthrough

Building on what's already in (Hopkuna remembers your RESETs):
- **After any ending, the voice at the start is different.**
  - After **Pacifist**, Hopkuna's voice is gone. Silence, then Relic, gentle: *"You came back. ...Did you forget something? Or someone?"* It's an invitation to leave things be, like Undertale's Flowey after the true ending.
  - After **Neutral**, Hopkuna: *"Walking away again? Of course you are."*
  - After **Genocide**, the voice is green: *"We remember."* And it never fully goes back. **Every later Pacifist ending has one change:** when Relic says goodbye, their eyes flicker red-green for one frame. The game remembers what you did.
- **Hop remembers a little, every time.** Each new playthrough, on meeting him: *"Elric. ...Huh. Sorry. You remind me of someone."* After a Genocide run, the pause is longer, and his hand drifts to his neck, to a cord that isn't there.

**The townsfolk** each carry a pain Relic once took from them, which is where their odd habits came from:
- **Pigeon Man:** grief for Geraldine, so now the pigeons are all Gerald.
- **The Waiting Ghost:** the ride that never came.
- **Coach Ramirez:** he keeps losing his keys because his son used to find them, and his son moved away.
- **The Dog Walker and Biscuit:** Biscuit was a puppy in the fire, and won't go near the middle of the field.
- **The Night Janitor:** he heard the bells ring on the night of the fire.

Returning their keepsakes is the quiet heart of Pacifist and Neutral, and killing them is the loudest part of Genocide.
